About me

A writer who learned to love data.
A strategist who never stopped writing.

My career started in the pages of educational publishing — a world that taught me that words carry weight, that audience matters, and that clarity is a form of respect. That foundation has shaped everything I've done since.

Over seven years across agency and in-house roles, I've grown into a lifecycle marketer who specializes in email and SMS strategy — mapping customer journeys, building automated workflows, and writing the copy that holds it all together. Currently, I lead email and SMS strategy at the School for International Training, driving outreach to prospective students, parents, partners, and alumni for a nonprofit with truly global reach.

I completed my MBA in Marketing from Hofstra University in 2025, which included a remarkable detour: as a Bioscience Business Innovation Fellow, I collaborated with Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory scientists to develop a cancer treatment business concept. It was a reminder that great communication — clear, empathetic, purposeful — matters in every industry.

When I'm not mapping out email sequences, you'll find me hunting for the perfect planner, making playlists for anyone who will accept them, painting watercolors, and spending quality time with my cat, Lincoln. I believe a curated playlist is an act of love — and I bring that same intentionality to every campaign I build.
